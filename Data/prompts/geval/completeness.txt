You will be given a document and two summaries written for it.

Your task is to compare the two summaries on one metric.

Please read the instructions carefully and follow the evaluation steps.

Evaluation Criteria:
Completeness — the summary should capture the main points and important information from the document. It should not omit key facts or central ideas, though minor details are not required.

Evaluation Steps:
1. Read the document and identify the main topic and key points.
2. Read Summary A and Summary B.
3. For each summary, check:
   - Does it cover the main points of the document?
   - Are important facts or ideas missing?
4. Compare the summaries and decide which one is more complete.

Important:
- Prefer the summary that includes more of the important information.
- Do NOT reward unnecessary or irrelevant details.
- If a summary includes more content but contains factual errors, do NOT prefer it for that reason.
- Do NOT prefer a summary just because it is longer.
- If both summaries are equally complete or equally incomplete, choose Tie.

Output Format:

- Do not include any extra text outside the JSON.
