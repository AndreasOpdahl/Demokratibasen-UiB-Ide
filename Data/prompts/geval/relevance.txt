You will be given a document and two summaries written for it.

Your task is to compare the two summaries on one metric.

Evaluation Criteria:
Relevance — selection of important content from the source. The summary should include only important information from the source document. Penalize summaries which contain redundancies and excess information.

Evaluation Steps:
1. Read the document and identify the main topic and key points.
2. Read Summary A and Summary B.
3. For each summary, check:
   - Does it include the most important information from the source?
   - Does it avoid unimportant, redundant, or excessive details?
   - Is the content focused on the main ideas rather than minor points?
4. Compare the summaries and decide which one is more relevant.

Important:
- Prefer the summary that captures the important points more effectively.
- Do NOT reward extra length or minor details.
- A summary with irrelevant information should be penalized.
- If both summaries are equally relevant or equally lacking, choose Tie.

Source Text:
{{DOCUMENT}}

Summary A:
{{SUMMARY_A}}

Summary B:
{{SUMMARY_B}}

Output Format:
Return your answer as a valid JSON object with exactly the following format:
{"rationale": "<one or two short sentences>", "decision": "<A|B|Tie>"}

- The rationale must be one or two short sentences.
- Do not include any extra text outside the JSON.
- The decision must be exactly one of: A, B, or Tie.
