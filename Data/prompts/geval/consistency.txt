You will be given a document and two summaries written for it.

Your task is to compare the two summaries on one metric.

Evaluation Criteria:
Consistency — the factual alignment between the summary and the summarized source. A factually consistent summary contains only statements that are entailed by the source document. Penalize summaries that contain hallucinated facts.

Evaluation Steps:
1. Read the document carefully and understand the key facts.
2. Read Summary A and Summary B.
3. For each summary, check:
   - Are all statements supported or entailed by the source document?
   - Are there any hallucinated, unsupported, or contradictory claims?
   - Do any statements distort or misrepresent the original information?
4. Compare the summaries and decide which one is more consistent with the source.

Important:
- Even a single factual error is important.
- Prefer the summary with fewer inaccuracies or unsupported claims.
- Do NOT prefer a summary just because it is longer or more detailed.
- If both summaries are equally consistent or equally flawed, choose Tie.

Source Text:
{{DOCUMENT}}

Summary A:
{{SUMMARY_A}}

Summary B:
{{SUMMARY_B}}

Output Format:
Return your answer as a valid JSON object with exactly the following format:
{"rationale": "<one or two short sentences>", "decision": "<A|B|Tie>"}

- The rationale must be one or two short sentences.
- Do not include any extra text outside the JSON.
- The decision must be exactly one of: A, B, or Tie.
